# Facilitator guide

Audience: STARS participants, ESIIL synthesis teams and interested educators. Adapt with appropriate partners. Participants should never be asked to disclose knowledge, represent a Nation, or approve research on its behalf.

## Session 01: questions and authority (75 minutes)

15 minutes: distinguish public evidence, proposed questions and actual agreements. 35 minutes: draft a research brief using one public case. 25 minutes: compare unresolved decisions.

Checkpoint: a college partnership is not automatically Tribal government research approval; a public source is not permission to reuse restricted knowledge. A blank plan should produce nine named gaps. A pending release record must fail the release check. There is no correct numerical readiness score.

## Session 02: fire history and detection (90 minutes)

20 minutes: inspect the data manifest, date coverage and type labels. 45 minutes: select one regional window, reproduce an event count and inspect the map. 25 minutes: discuss alternative explanations.

Checkpoint: the event total in the annual CSV must equal the number of distinct event IDs in the regional GeoJSON and manifest. Counts are assigned to a rectangle, not a Nation. A zero is no mapped event in that snapshot/year/type cell. It does not establish no fire or no stewardship. Reburns make summed event area different from unique land area burned. “Prescribed Fire” does not identify cultural fire.

## Session 03: stewardship and exchange (75 minutes)

15 minutes: source review. 35 minutes: compare two cases. 25 minutes: draft an invitation question and exchange terms.

Checkpoint: every claim about a program has a source. Proposed questions are identified as the author's suggestions. Yurok and Karuk authorities and histories remain distinct. Southern Ute's published CU collaboration should be investigated before proposing overlapping work. The BIA agency listing does not identify Ute Mountain Ute priorities. A useful shared lesson can concern sustained staffing or host-defined measures; an answer about cultural meanings belongs to the relevant holders.

## Session 04: monitoring outcomes (90 minutes)

20 minutes: design and units. 45 minutes: protocol and analysis validation. 25 minutes: interpretation.

Hypothetical arithmetic answer: (14−10)−(9−8)=3 units. This is not an estimate for a real treatment. Drought, seasonality, different baselines and treatment selection can create contrasts. Four rows are one pair, not four replicates. Repeated pixels do not establish independent treatments. One pre-period cannot test parallel trends. No confidence interval is provided below ten independent pairs; that threshold does not by itself establish valid inference.

Technical checkpoints: duplicate unit-period cells, reused units, mixed units/methods, incomplete pairs, unresolved quality flags and reversed dates must fail. A completed protocol and partner-chosen data handling are needed before interpreting any real observations.

## Suggested assessment

Evaluate clear questions, correct units, traceable evidence, honest uncertainty and reproducibility. Offer a non-coding path through the same reasoning. Do not assess cultural knowledge.
