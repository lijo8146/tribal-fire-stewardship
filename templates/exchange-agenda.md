# Draft learning exchange agenda

This is a planning worksheet.                                       

## Before an invitation

- Ask whether the proposed exchange would serve a purpose hosts value.
- Identify the appropriate Tribal, organizational and practitioner authorities separately.
- Agree on compensation, travel support, preparation time, accessibility and desired benefits.
- Establish what may be discussed, recorded, photographed, summarized or shared further.

## Session prompts for hosts to revise

1. **Purpose and relationships:** what would make this exchange useful?
2. **How success is defined:** which outcomes matter to the people doing the work?
3. **Sustaining stewardship:** authority, access, staffing, training, equipment and funding.
4. **Useful evidence:** observations that help; data products that miss important purposes.
5. **What travels:** ideas to adapt, questions to investigate and knowledge that remains specific.
6. **Closing and next steps:** agree on any learning record, review, attribution and follow-up.

A field visit or practitioner demonstration is considered only if hosts invite it and set the terms. This agenda contains no ignition procedures or burn prescriptions.
